# Children Rules

## Purpose

Child classification rules.

## Business Rules

BR-0002 | Age Groups

Values: Three AG:

- Infant
- Toddler
- Preschool

---

BR-0003 | Infant

Rule: Birth date > 1 June of the year preceding the SY.

---

BR-0004 | Toddler

Rule: Birth date:

1 September (SY -2)
->
31 May (SY -1)

---

BR-0005 | Preschool

Rule: Birth date:

1 January (SY -2)
->
1 September (SY -2)

Notes: Children older than this normally belong to kindergarten.

---

BR-0006 | Age Exception

Rule: Children born before the Preschool range may remain in daycare only with special approval.

---

BR-0007 | Private Daycare

Rule: Private daycare uses the same age classification rules.

Values: Groups:

- Private Infant
- Private Toddler
- Private Preschool

---
