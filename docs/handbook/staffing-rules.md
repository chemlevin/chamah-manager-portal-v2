# Staffing Rules

## Purpose

Staffing and licensing rules.

## Business Rules

BR-0014 | Staffing Ratio

Rule: Staffing ratio is determined by:
- LT
- AG

---

BR-0015 | Staffing Rounding

Rule: Round only after calculation.

Values:
0.01-0.50 -> +0.5

0.51-0.99 -> +1

---

BR-0022 | Operating Hours

Rule: Operating hours are defined per SY.

Notes: Historical values remain unchanged.

---

BR-0023 | Monthly Licensing Hours

Rule: LicensingHours are calculated for each month using:
- Operating Hours
- Working Days (Sun-Thu)
- Fridays

Notes: LicensingHours vary every month.

---

BR-0024 | Required Licensing Hours

Formula: Required Licensing Hours = LicensingHours x Required Staffing

---

BR-0025 | Daycare Licensing Hours

Formula: Total Daycare Licensing Hours = Sum of LicensingHours of all classrooms.

---
