# Classroom Rules

## Purpose

Classroom rules.

## Business Rules

BR-0016 | Area

Rule: Area/child depends on AG.

---

BR-0017 | Capacity

Rule: Max children depends on AG.

Notes: Area cannot override maximum capacity.

---

BR-0018 | Area Tolerance

Rule: Shortage <1 m2 = Allowed.

Notes: Shortage >=1 m2 = Not Allowed.

---

BR-0019 | Mixed Classroom

Values: Allowed:
- Infant + Toddler
- Toddler + Preschool

Not Allowed:
- Infant + Preschool

---

BR-0020 | Mixed Area

Rule: Calculate each AG separately.

Formula: Total Area = Sum.

---

BR-0021 | Mixed Staffing

Rule: Calculate each AG separately.

Formula: Sum first. Round once.

---
