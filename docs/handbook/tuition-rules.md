# Tuition Rules

## Purpose

Tuition rules.

## Business Rules

BR-0008 | Tuition Categories

Values: Licensed:
- Extended
- Basic

Private:
- Infant
- Toddler
- Preschool

---

BR-0009 | Legal Entity

Rule: Licensed tuition depends on LE:

- Nonprofit
- Company

Notes: Private tuition is independent from LE.

---

BR-0010 | School Year

Rule: Tuition values are defined per SY.

Notes: Historical values are never overwritten.

---

BR-0011 | Licensed Tuition Selection

Rule: Licensed tuition is determined by:

- SY
- LE
- Licensing Category
- AG

---

BR-0012 | Private Tuition Selection

Rule: Private tuition is determined by:

- SY
- Private AG

---

BR-0013 | Tuition Persistence

Rule: Changing tuition for a new SY must not modify previous SYs.

Notes: Historical tuition remains immutable.

---
