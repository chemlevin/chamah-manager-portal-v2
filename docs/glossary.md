# Terminology

## SY (Educational School Year)

Educational year.
1 September -> 31 August.

---

## CY (Calendar Year)

Financial year.
1 January -> 31 December.

---

## AG (Age Group)

- Infant
- Toddler
- Preschool

---

## LT (Licensing Type)

- Extended
- Basic

---

## LE (Legal Entity)

- Nonprofit
- Company

---

## RD (Reference Data)

Configuration values that may change over time without changing Business Rules.

---

## LicensingHours

Hours required for licensing calculations.

---

## PlanningHours

Hours used as a planning assumption.

---

## HourlyRate

Rate used for hourly payroll calculations.

---

## Headcount

Employee count used for planning.

---

## Rules

Use these abbreviations consistently across all documentation created from this point forward.

Do not rename abbreviations without updating this glossary.
